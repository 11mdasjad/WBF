# Photography and asset credits

## Current photographic design — 26 October 2026

The public website now uses real photographs, with WBF program photographs in the focus cards and licensed illustrative photography in selected banners. Former design-reference collages and illustrations are retired from the public interface. Legacy CMS image references are mapped to photographs at display time without changing saved content. Historical asset notes below document earlier versions, not the current layout.

The home, education and volunteering photographs were downloaded again from the same credited Unsplash sources at 2400 pixels wide. Their subjects and proportions are preserved; CSS crops them responsively. No generated replacement imagery was used. Existing WBF program photographs retain their original source resolution.

All seven photographs below were downloaded from the free Unsplash image catalog on 25 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata) and visually inspected. They are illustrative stock photography, **not documentary evidence of WBF programs or identified WBF beneficiaries, volunteers, employees, or partners**. Do not attach fabricated quotes, names, program outcomes, or medical conditions to the people shown. Gallery and image captions must preserve this distinction.

The [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) permits free downloading, copying, modifying, distribution and commercial use. Attribution is appreciated. Search-result entries for these photographs provided ordinary download links rather than Unsplash+ purchase links. The license does not confer rights to imply endorsement or misuse likenesses; these assets are used as general thematic illustrations.
